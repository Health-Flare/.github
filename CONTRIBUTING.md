# Helping Health Flare

Health Flare makes free, open source apps for tracking chronic illness (Health Flare) and your cycle (Inner Flare). No account, no analytics, no server of ours. It's built by one developer, so most of what it needs isn't code.

If you help, you get credited. Every contribution, code or not, gets your name (or the handle you choose) on [healthflare.org/thanks](https://healthflare.org/thanks/) and in the project's `CONTRIBUTORS.md`. See [Getting credited](#getting-credited).

## Ways to help

### Design

The logo, icons and site imagery were made with AI tools. We'd like a human designer to replace them: logo and wordmark, app icon, UI review, and graphics for the website and store listings.

Start by opening an issue in [website](https://github.com/Health-Flare/website/issues) saying what you'd like to take on. Share drafts as images in the issue; no design tool access needed.

Artwork you contribute is licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), so anyone can reuse it with credit to you. The Health Flare and Inner Flare names and logos stay the project's marks: you can't use them to brand something else.

### Marketing and outreach

Ad campaigns, social posts, short videos, handouts for clinics and support groups. Anything that helps the right people hear about the apps.

One rule matters more than the rest: **only say what's true.** Use claims the site already makes ("No account, no analytics, no server of ours", "Free and open source"). Don't say data "never leaves your device" or "stays on your phone" (phone backups include it), and nothing that sounds like diagnosis or treatment. No tracking pixels on anything that links to healthflare.org. Ask in an issue if you're not sure.

### Writing and editing

Blog posts, store listings, help text, and plain-language review of what's already there. If a sentence in the app or on the site confused you, that's worth an issue on its own.

### Medical and clinical review

Clinicians, nurses, pharmacists and researchers: check the wording, the defaults and the sources. Inner Flare cites a source for every health estimate it shows; tell us if one is wrong or out of date. Open an issue in [app](https://github.com/Health-Flare/app/issues) or [InnerFlare](https://github.com/Health-Flare/InnerFlare/issues).

### Living with it

If you track your own illness or care for someone who does, tell us how the app holds up on a bad day. What took too long, what you skipped, what you wished it asked. You don't need to share anything about your health to do this.

### Testing and bug reports

Install from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/health-flare/id6803123766), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.healthflare.app.healthflare), or the APK on each repo's Releases page, then use the bug report template in the repo's issues.

### Accessibility

Screen reader, large text, contrast, one-handed use. Report what breaks, with your device and settings.

### Translation

Not ready yet: the apps still have their text hard-coded in English. Open an issue saying which language you could help with, and we'll come to you when translation opens.

### Open Health Index

Know a good open source health tool? Suggest it at [openhealthindex.org](https://openhealthindex.org) using the "Suggest a tool" issue template in [open-health-index](https://github.com/Health-Flare/open-health-index/issues).

### Code

Each repo has its own `CONTRIBUTING.md` with setup and rules: [app](https://github.com/Health-Flare/app/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md), [InnerFlare](https://github.com/Health-Flare/InnerFlare/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md), [open-health-index](https://github.com/Health-Flare/open-health-index/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Getting credited

Code is credited automatically by GitHub. Everything else is credited by hand, in two places: on the public [thanks page](https://healthflare.org/thanks/), and in `CONTRIBUTORS.md` in the repo the work was for:

- app work: [app/CONTRIBUTORS.md](https://github.com/Health-Flare/app/blob/main/CONTRIBUTORS.md)
- Inner Flare: [InnerFlare/CONTRIBUTORS.md](https://github.com/Health-Flare/InnerFlare/blob/main/CONTRIBUTORS.md)
- website, design, marketing and outreach: [website/CONTRIBUTORS.md](https://github.com/Health-Flare/website/blob/main/CONTRIBUTORS.md)

One line per person, describing the work, not the person. You're only listed if you agree, under the name you choose, and you're removed if you ask. We never mention anyone's health or family unless they ask us to.

## Talking to us

Open an issue in the repo that fits, or start with [website](https://github.com/Health-Flare/website/issues) if you're not sure. Security or privacy problems: don't open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/Health-Flare/app/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

Everything here follows the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/Health-Flare/app/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
