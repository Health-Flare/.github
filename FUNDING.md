# Funding

This org builds and maintains free, open-source software — currently
[HealthFlare](https://github.com/Health-Flare/app) and
[Inner Flare](https://github.com/Health-Flare/InnerFlare), both privacy-first
health tracking apps.

If one of these projects is useful to you and you're able to, you can support
their ongoing development:

- **[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/Health-Flare)** — no fees,
  one-time or recurring.
- **[Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/health-flare)** — a public
  ledger of every dollar in and out. Use this if you want to see exactly
  where the money goes before giving.

## Where the money goes, in order of priority

1. **Hosting & infrastructure** — CI minutes, app store developer accounts
   (Apple/Google), domain renewals, any backend services the apps depend on.
2. **Tooling & dependencies** — paid services or licenses that materially
   improve the software (e.g. code signing, crash reporting, translation
   services).
3. **Maintainer time** — once recurring funding covers (1) and (2) with
   headroom, a portion goes toward compensating maintainer time. This is
   currently the last priority, not the first.

## What sponsorship does *not* buy

Sponsoring doesn't get you priority support, guaranteed feature delivery, or
an SLA. Feature requests and bug reports are triaged the same way regardless
of sponsorship status. If that ever changes (e.g. a paid support tier), it
will be announced here first.

## Transparency

- **Open Collective** publishes every transaction automatically — see the
  [collective's page](https://opencollective.com/health-flare) for the live
  ledger, or the per-project pages for
  [HealthFlare](https://opencollective.com/health-flare/projects/app)
  and [Inner Flare](https://opencollective.com/health-flare/projects/innerflare).
- We also publish a short **quarterly update** (income, expenses, what
  shipped as a result) — see [`TRANSPARENCY.md`](./TRANSPARENCY.md) and past
  entries linked from there.

## Is this a company or a person?

Funds raised through this org account are pooled for project costs (hosting,
tooling, app store fees) rather than routed to any one individual's personal
income. This is not (yet) a registered nonprofit or legal entity — see
`TRANSPARENCY.md` for the current operating structure and any changes to it.
