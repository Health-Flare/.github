# Health Flare <img src="https://github.com/Health-Flare/app/blob/main/assets/images/HealthFlare%20Icon%20Light-256.png" width="48" height="48">

**Free, open source apps for tracking chronic illness and reproductive health. No account, no analytics, no server of ours.**

Built for the people doing the tracking, whether that's for yourself or for someone you look after, and for the days you have the least energy to do it.

## The apps

**[Health Flare](https://github.com/Health-Flare/app)** tracks symptoms, vitals, medications, meals, sleep, flares and appointments. One install can hold several profiles, so a parent can track more than one child. Before an appointment you can add your questions and take a PDF report of the period that matters.

- [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/health-flare/id6803123766) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.healthflare.app.healthflare) · [healthflare.org](https://healthflare.org)

**[Inner Flare](https://github.com/Health-Flare/InnerFlare)** tracks periods, symptoms and cycles. Its database is encrypted on the device. Every health estimate it shows cites a source.

- [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.healthflare.app.innerflare) · [healthflare.org/inner-flare](https://healthflare.org/inner-flare/)

**[Open Health Index](https://github.com/Health-Flare/open-health-index)** is a list of open source health tools, ours and other people's: [openhealthindex.org](https://openhealthindex.org).

## What we promise, and what we don't

- No account and no sign-in. No analytics, ads or tracking SDKs.
- Your records are on your phone, not on a server of ours. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) includes them, the same as your other apps. See the [privacy policy](https://healthflare.org/privacy) for how those backups are protected.
- Health Flare's only network call is an opt-in weather lookup, which sends an approximate location to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com).
- Backups you export can be password-encrypted.
- Not a medical device. It keeps a record; it doesn't diagnose.

Every feature starts as a plain-language spec in the repo (`docs/features/`), so you can read what the app is supposed to do before you trust it with anything.
